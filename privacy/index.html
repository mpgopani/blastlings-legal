Blastlings Privacy Policy

Effective 4 November 2026 · Applies to the Blastlings game for Android (com.blastlings.game)

Blastlings is made by MP Gopani (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Blastlings. The short version: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the game keeps, and where

Your progress is saved only on your own device: the levels you have finished and their stars, the coins and gems you have earned by playing, your lives, the outfits you own and your settings. This file never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in, and no name, email address, phone number or contact list is ever asked for.
  • No advertising, and no advertising ID is read.
  • No analytics or tracking of how you play.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins and gems are earned in the game and cannot be bought or sold.
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos or files are used.
  • No chat, online play or sharing with other players.

Permissions

The app asks for two ordinary Android permissions: vibration, for haptic feedback (the Vibration slider in Settings turns it down or off), and internet access, which the game engine (Unity) includes by default. The game does not use it to send information about you.

Google Play

You download and update Blastlings through Google Play. Google may collect information about your device and app use under its own privacy policy, for example crash reports that Android sends to Google Play when the app stops unexpectedly. Google shows us those reports in the Play Console so we can fix bugs; they do not tell us who you are. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Blastlings does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), because it collects none at all.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds something that uses personal information, such as cloud saves, purchases, analytics or ads, we will update this policy before that version is released, change the effective date above, and update the game's data-safety information on Google Play.

Contact
