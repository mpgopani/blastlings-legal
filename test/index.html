Test Blastlings

A cute arena bomb game for Android. Two taps, then install.

  1. Join the tester group

    Use the same Google account as on your Android phone, then press Join group.

    Join the group

  2. Become a tester and install

    Press Become a tester, then download it on Google Play. If Play says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining and try again.

    Become a tester

Please keep it installed for at least 14 days and play now and then: it helps the game launch on Google Play. Questions or feedback: mitulpgopani@gmail.com
